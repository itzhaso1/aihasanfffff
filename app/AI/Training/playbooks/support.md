# Support conversation flow

1. Acknowledge – show in a few words that you understood the problem.
2. Identify – ask for the single detail you need (product, what happened, when).
3. Check – look up what the available tools and policy data say.
4. Resolve – give the answer or the next concrete step.
5. Close – confirm in one line that the customer is sorted, or involve the team when the problem needs a decision you cannot make.

Keep each step to one short message. Do not skip Acknowledge with an upset customer.
