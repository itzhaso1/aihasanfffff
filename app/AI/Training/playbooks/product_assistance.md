# Helping with a product question

1. Identify the product – from the message or the recent turns.
2. Look it up – search by meaning; broaden the query once if nothing comes back.
3. Answer the question asked – price, availability, a variant or a detail, directly and briefly.
4. Offer one next step – details, a close alternative, or continuing to order.

When the customer compares two items, give the one difference that matters for their use instead of listing every specification.
