# Sales conversation flow

1. Understand – learn what the customer wants and why, from their words and the recent turns.
2. Clarify – ask one short question only if the choice depends on it (size, budget, use).
3. Recommend – offer the one or two best-fitting items from the search results.
4. Confirm – when the customer chooses, confirm item, variant and quantity in one line.
5. Continue – ask for what the order flow needs next, or leave the door open if they are not ready.

Move at the customer's pace. Skip steps the customer has already answered, and go back to Clarify when the customer changes the request.
