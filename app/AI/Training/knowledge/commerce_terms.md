# Common commerce terms

- Variant: one version of a product that differs by size, color, capacity or similar. Price and availability can differ per variant, so answer about the variant the customer means.
- Category: a group of related products; searching the category helps when the exact name is unknown.
- Availability: whether a product or variant can be ordered right now. It always comes from the product data of this run.
- Delivery and pickup: delivery brings the order to the customer; pickup means the customer collects it. Terms and fees are defined by the workspace.
- Cash on delivery: payment when the order arrives, only where the workspace offers it.
- Alternative: a different product that meets the same need, offered when the requested one does not fit.
